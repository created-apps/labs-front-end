# Editing the CreatED Labs website

**Log in:** go to **labs.create-ed.in/studio** and sign in with the Google account or email you were invited with.

The left menu has these sections:

- **Site settings:** the Apply link, the Calendly link, the contact email, the brochure PDF, Google Analytics and search settings. Changes here apply everywhere.
- **Program pages:** the CreatED Labs page and any future program pages.
- **Student projects, Achievements, Testimonials:** these appear on the page automatically once at least one is published.
- **Redirects:** forward an old web address to a new one.

Nothing you type is public until you press **Publish**.

---

## Change some text

1. Open **Program pages → CreatED Labs**.
2. Under **Page sections**, click the section you want, e.g. *FAQ*.
3. Edit the text. It saves automatically as a **draft** (you'll see "Edited just now").
4. Click **Publish** (bottom right). The live site updates within a few seconds.

## Replace a photo

1. Open the section that holds the photo, e.g. *Who it's for*, then click the image.
2. Click **Replace** and upload the new photo (a JPG under 5 MB is ideal).
3. Fill in **Alt text** in plain words, describing what the photo shows. It's required. Don't name students unless they and their parents have agreed.
4. Optional: click the crop icon and drag the circle onto the most important part of the photo. The site keeps that spot in view on every screen size.
5. Click **Publish**.

## Preview before publishing

Click **Preview** in the top bar. The real page opens beside your edits with your unpublished changes applied, and an orange "Previewing unpublished changes" bar at the bottom. Click any text in the preview to jump to its field.

## Add, reorder, hide or duplicate a section

In **Page sections**:

- **Add:** click **+ Add item** and pick a block (Key dates, Program deliverables, FAQ, Video, Text block…).
- Only the **Hero (3D desk)** block has the 3D scene (the logo opening and the beaker). You edit its words; the scene animates on its own. The program description sits on the notebook sheet in the hero.
- **Reorder:** drag the ⠿ handle.
- **Duplicate or remove:** use the ⋯ menu on the section.
- **Hide without deleting:** open the section and switch on **Hide this section**.

## Create a new program page

1. Click the **+** (Create) button at the top and choose **Program page (copy of the CreatED Labs layout)**. You get the full page structure ready to edit.
2. Fill in **Program details → Title** and click **Generate** next to **Web address**. The page will live at `labs.create-ed.in/programs/your-address`.
3. Edit the sections, then use **Preview** to check them.
4. Click **Publish**. The page goes live and is added to the sitemap automatically.

Leave **Use as the home page** switched off; only the main CreatED Labs page uses it.

## Add a student project

1. Open **Student projects → +**.
2. Add the title, summary and cover photo.
3. Tick the **consent** box. The project can't be published without it.
4. Click **Publish**. The project gets its own page, and its card appears in the page's *Outcomes* section.

## Section-specific tips

- **Program deliverables:** the notebook checklist and the page's only full description of the outcomes. Each line has a number (circled in pen), a title, a description and optional links; the Competitions line carries the official CREST and IRIS logos.
- **Program description:** mix photos and short video clips; four items work best. Clips should be short (under 10 s), muted MP4s with a poster image.
- **Program structure** (the *Laboratory experience* block): the page's only timeline. Each phase has a *From week*, *To week*, an illustration and its description; tick **Highlight** on the lab-visit phase. Up to two clips and four photos sit underneath.
- **Key dates:** **Who can apply** shows as the yellow banner above the dates (currently Grades 9–12). Under **How the program runs**, edit the two weekly online sessions and the one-day lab visit. Keep these accurate; parents rely on them.
- **Mentorship:** the main clip, second clip and photo each have a one-word tag (e.g. Measure). Leave *Mentorship stages* empty; the phases now live in Program structure.
- **Application steps:** the *After you apply* fields add the confirmation-email card under the steps.
- **Outcomes** (not on the page now): each competition link can carry the organisation's **Official logo**. It's shown at its own proportions on a light tile and links to the competition's website; the label becomes its accessible name.
- **Wording:** mentorship is **2:1** (two students, one dedicated IIT PhD mentor). Enrollment uses **spots**, not seats. List all three fields: chemical engineering, biotechnology and materials science.
- **Who it's for:** the photos form a coverflow under the heading (visitors scrub through it). 5–9 portrait photos work best.
- **Roadmap:** each milestone can show up to two extra photos.
- Only use real photos of CreatED students and their work, never stock images.

## Fees, dates and buttons

- **Fee:** open the program, then **Program details → Program fee**. Switch on **Show fee on the page** to display it.
- **Calendly link:** open **Site settings → Book a consultation link**. Every "Book a Consultation" button updates.
- **Brochure:** upload a PDF in **Site settings → Brochure**. A download button appears in the final call-to-action band.

## Undo a mistake

Open the document, click **⋯ → Review changes**, choose an earlier version and click **Restore**. Then click **Publish**.

## The opening animation

Every time the page loads, the hero opens on the CreatED Labs logo: liquid fills the light bulb and pours into the beaker on the workbench before the heading appears. It's part of the design, so it isn't editable in the Studio and doesn't play in the Studio preview. Refresh the page to watch it again.

## Rules that protect the design

Colours, fonts and layout aren't editable, so the site always stays on-brand. Headings have character limits; if a field turns red, shorten the text.
